# PractiSci: virtual physics and chemistry labs for Class 11 and 12

[PractiSci](https://www.practisci.in) is a virtual lab platform with 400+ interactive 3D experiments for Class 11 and Class 12 Physics and Chemistry (NCERT, NEET and JEE level), in English and Hindi. Students change the apparatus, take readings, plot live graphs, answer viva questions and follow guided steps, on a phone or a laptop.

Website: **https://www.practisci.in**

## What you can do there
- **Physics labs:** Ohm's law, metre bridge, potentiometer, Young's double slit, photoelectric effect, projectile motion, Carnot engine and more. See the [lab catalogue](https://www.practisci.in/labs).
- **Chemistry labs:** titration, salt analysis, electrochemistry, atoms and bonds, and a [periodic table game](https://www.practisci.in/play/periodic-table).
- **Chapter guides** with formulas, worked examples and FAQs: [ray optics](https://www.practisci.in/topics/ray-optics), [wave optics](https://www.practisci.in/topics/wave-optics), [current electricity](https://www.practisci.in/topics/current-electricity), [electromagnetic induction](https://www.practisci.in/topics/electromagnetic-induction), [thermodynamics](https://www.practisci.in/topics/thermodynamics), [projectile motion](https://www.practisci.in/topics/projectile-motion-kinematics), [work, energy and power](https://www.practisci.in/topics/work-energy-power) and [rotational motion](https://www.practisci.in/topics/rotational-motion).
- **Practical lists by class:** [Class 11 physics](https://www.practisci.in/practicals/class-11-physics), [Class 12 physics](https://www.practisci.in/practicals/class-12-physics), [Class 11 chemistry](https://www.practisci.in/practicals/class-11-chemistry), [Class 12 chemistry](https://www.practisci.in/practicals/class-12-chemistry).
